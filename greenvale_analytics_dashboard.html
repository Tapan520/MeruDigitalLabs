Headline insight: Days-since-last-activity is by far the strongest churn predictor (r = 0.87) — customers inactive 31–60 days churn at 80.5%, and 60+ days at 100%, versus <1% for customers active in the last two weeks. This is a near-perfect early-warning trigger, and it's cheap to compute — no ML model required to act on it on day one.

Headline insight: The bottleneck isn't document intake (2.0 days, already efficient) — it's analyst review (6.9 days, backlog-driven) and committee review (4.6 days, meets only on Tuesdays), together 75% of the 15.3-day average. A pre-screening triage model plus a shift to twice-weekly / delegated-authority committee decisions is the fastest path to the 10-day target — not a rebuild of the whole process.

Regulatory flag: Only 14.5% of overlapping customers have a documented consent record, and Greenvale's privacy notice does not yet reference Covera. The CDO's "shared Group data" framing conflicts with Group Legal's SYSC 8 / UK GDPR position. Recommendation: treat cross-entity data use as gated behind either (a) a documented Legitimate Interest Assessment for aggregate/model-level use, or (b) explicit opt-in consent for customer-level personalisation — with the privacy notice and consent capture fixed before any data product ships.
